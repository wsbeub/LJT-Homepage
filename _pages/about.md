Hello! I’m Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with interests in large language model reasoning, reinforcement learning, truthfulness, interpretability, and hallucination issues in vision-language models.

Academic background

- Ph.D. in Computer Science, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2024–Present)
- B.Eng., Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2020–2024)

Research experience

- Research Intern, MINIMAX (Feb 2025–Present)
- Research Intern, Tencent WXG (Jun 2024–Sep 2024)
- Research Intern, Shanghai AI Lab (Jun 2023–Dec 2023)

Skills

- Machine learning and NLP research
- Large language models, reasoning, and evaluation
- Vision-language models and hallucination analysis
